A subscription is a recurring bill: Solverr creates a new invoice for the customer every week or month. Crypto wallets cannot be charged automatically, so the customer pays each invoice on the normal checkout page.

## Create a subscription

In **Dashboard → Subscriptions**, enter the customer's email, the amount, the currency, the interval and an optional description, then select **Create subscription**.

To create one with the API, send `POST /api/v1/subscriptions`:

```bash
curl https://your-solverr-host/api/v1/subscriptions \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer sk_live_…" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "customer_email": "ada@example.com",
    "amount": "9.00",
    "currency": "USD",
    "interval": "month",
    "description": "Pro plan"
  }'
```

```json
{
  "id": "sub_2mQ8vT4kW9xL3pN7rB5s",
  "object": "subscription",
  "status": "active",
  "customer_email": "ada@example.com",
  "amount": "9.00",
  "currency": "USD",
  "interval": "month",
  "description": "Pro plan",
  "next_invoice_at": "2026-11-05T12:00:00.000Z",
  "created_at": "2026-11-05T12:00:00.000Z"
}
```

`GET /api/v1/subscriptions` lists your subscriptions, newest first.

### Limits

Each subscription sends email, so a business can create at most 500 subscriptions in 24 hours and keep at most 2,000 active. Until its first payment, a new business can create at most 10 subscriptions in 24 hours. Past either limit, the API answers `409 invalid_state`. Cancel subscriptions you no longer need.

### Fields

| Field | Rules |
|---|---|
| `customer_email` | Required, a valid email address |
| `amount` | Required, a decimal string with up to 2 decimals |
| `currency` | Required, `USD`, `EUR` or `INR` |
| `interval` | Required, `week` or `month` |
| `description` | Optional, up to 200 characters. Without it, invoices say "Weekly subscription" or "Monthly subscription" |

## What happens each period

1. The worker checks subscriptions every minute, so the first invoice is created within about a minute.
2. Each invoice stays payable for 24 hours. You get a `subscription.invoice_created` webhook with the invoice.
3. When email is configured, the customer gets an email with the payment link. Otherwise, send them the invoice's `checkout_url` yourself. One email address gets at most 10 payment-link emails in 24 hours across all businesses. Past that, the email is skipped and you send the `checkout_url` yourself.
4. The next invoice is due one week later, or on the same day of the next month. When the next month is shorter, for example after a subscription created on January 31, the invoice is due on that month's last day, and later months return to the original day.

Each invoice works like any other: you get `invoice.paid` when it is paid, or `invoice.expired` when the customer did not pay within 24 hours. A missed invoice does not stop the subscription.

## Cancel a subscription

In **Dashboard → Subscriptions**, select **Cancel** next to the subscription. Its status becomes `canceled` and no more invoices are created. Invoices already sent stay payable until they expire.

With the API, `DELETE /api/v1/subscriptions/:id` cancels a subscription and returns it with `"status": "canceled"`.
